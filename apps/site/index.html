Questions

Things studios ask us.

Do guests need to install an app or make an account?

No. The gallery opens in the phone’s browser. Guests type the PIN you shared and they’re in. You can ask them for a name and phone number first if you want to know who visited.

Can I bring in photos that are already in Google Drive?

Yes. Connect Google Drive once, then pick folders or photos in Google’s own window. Each folder becomes an album. Frameline only sees the files you pick. You can also keep the full-size files in your own Drive.

Will my photos lose quality?

Guests browse a sharp 2048 px copy that loads fast on mobile data. Your originals stay as you uploaded them, and you decide whether guests can download them full-size.

How long does a gallery stay open?

A year from the event date (or from when you create it, for an event already past). After that you can renew it for another year in one click.

Can guests download every photo?

You choose, per event: web-size, full-size, whole albums at once, or no downloads. Downloads carry your watermark if you turn it on.

What happens after the free trial?

Message us on WhatsApp and we set up the plan that fits. Your galleries stay open either way.
