Frameline Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026. "Frameline" is a working product name and may change; this policy covers the service regardless of its final name.

Draft. This policy is a working draft that describes what the product does today. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer; before a public launch it will be checked against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and any other place the service operates in.

Frameline ("we", "us") is a photo-delivery service run by Grateful World Ventures (OPC) Private Limited (registered address and GSTIN to be added). It lets event photographers ("studios") upload photos from a shoot and share them with their clients and guests. This page explains what personal data we collect, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it.

Who this applies to

Two kinds of people use Frameline: studios (photographers and their team, who have an account) and guests (people invited to a studio's event gallery, who usually don't have an account). A studio decides what its guests see and how long their event stays open; for the photos and guest details tied to one event, the studio is responsible for them and we handle them on its behalf. For a studio's own account data, we are responsible.

What we collect

  • Studio accounts: name, email, phone, the billing details you add for invoices (name, address, GSTIN), and, if you sell photos through us later, business and payout bank details.
  • Guest details: name, mobile number and (if you add it) email, given when a guest opens a gallery that asks for them.
  • Event photos a studio uploads, and photos a guest uploads back (if a studio allows it). We also keep a smaller web-size copy of each photo so galleries load quickly.
  • Selfies and face data: not collected at the moment. Face matching ("Find my photos with a selfie") is switched off. We may offer it later; if we do, it will only run when a guest chooses to take a selfie, the face data will be used only to find that guest's photos in that one event, and this policy will say so before it starts.
  • Order and payment information once selling photos is switched on (it is not yet): name, shipping address for prints, and order amount. Card and UPI details would be handled directly by our payment processor (Razorpay); we never see or store your card number.
  • Support questions a studio sends from Help, and the answers.
  • Basic technical data: IP address and device information, kept briefly for security, abuse prevention (e.g. limiting repeated PIN attempts) and error diagnosis.
  • Account sign-in: if you use "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us, under Google's own privacy policy.

Why we collect it

  • To run the service: create your studio, store and deliver your event photos, and let guests open, browse and download them.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To send the emails you'd expect: sign-in codes, team and host invites, and links to photo downloads.
  • To answer studios' support questions.
  • Later, once they are switched on: to process orders and pay studios that sell through us.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use guest photos or contact details for advertising.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers to run Frameline. Each only receives the data it needs to do its job:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, file storage and our database. All photos and account data are stored with Cloudflare.
  • Google (Gmail / Google Workspace): sending our emails (the recipient's address and the email itself), and sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Groq and NVIDIA: the assistant that answers support questions in Help. The question, the conversation so far and a few facts about the studio's account needed to answer it (plan, wallet, the event's settings) are sent to them to write a reply. Guests' details are not sent.
  • Studio AutoPilot: only when a studio links its Studio AutoPilot account. Then Studio AutoPilot can sign the studio in, create galleries for its projects, and read each gallery's name, link, PINs, photo and visit counts and closing date, plus the studio's plan, wallet balance and photos used. Guest details are not shared with it.
  • Razorpay: payment processing, once payments are switched on.

We don't share personal data with anyone else, except where required by law.

How long we keep it

  • A studio's account data is kept while the account is active, and for a reasonable period after in case you want to reactivate it.
  • An event's photos and guest data are kept for as long as the studio keeps that event. When a studio deletes an event (or a photo, or an album), it's held for 30 days so it can be restored if deleted by mistake, then permanently removed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent you gave. For anything to do with a specific event's guest data, the fastest route is asking that event's studio directly, since they control the gallery; for anything about your own studio account, or if you'd rather come to us, email dev@gratefulworldventures.in and we'll act on it.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences (like a collapsed menu). We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

Frameline is meant for use by event photographers and their adult clients and guests. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children without appropriate consent.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
