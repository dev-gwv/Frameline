Frameline Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026. "Frameline" is a working product name and may change; this policy covers the service regardless of its final name.

Frameline ("we", "us") is a photo-delivery service that lets event photographers ("studios") upload photos from a shoot and share them with their clients and guests, including finding a guest's own photos with a selfie. This page explains what personal data we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Who this applies to

Two kinds of people use Frameline: studios (photographers and their team, who have an account) and guests (people invited to a studio's event gallery, who usually don't have an account). A studio decides what its guests see and how long their event stays open; for the photos and guest details tied to one event, the studio is the data controller and we act as its processor. For a studio's own account data, we are the controller.

What we collect

  • Studio accounts: name, email, phone, business details you provide for selling photos (e.g. GST/PAN for invoicing), and payout bank details if you sell photos through us.
  • Guest details: name and an email or phone number, given when a guest opens a gallery that asks for them.
  • Selfie photos, only if a guest chooses to use "Find my photos": we process the selfie to compute a mathematical representation of the face (a set of numbers we can compare against event photos) so the guest can find photos they're in. We don't use this to identify anyone outside the one event it was taken for, and it is not shared, sold, or used for any other purpose.
  • Event photos a studio uploads, and photos a guest uploads back (if a studio allows it).
  • Order and payment information if a guest buys photos or prints: name, shipping address for prints, and order amount. Card and UPI details are handled directly by our payment processor (Razorpay) — we never see or store your card number.
  • Basic technical data: IP address and device information, kept briefly for security, abuse prevention (e.g. rate-limiting), and error diagnosis.
  • Account sign-in: if you use "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us, under Google's own privacy policy.

Why we collect it

  • To run the service: create your studio, store and deliver your event photos, and let guests find and download the photos they're in.
  • To process orders when a guest buys photos or prints, and to pay studios that sell through us.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (for example, limiting repeated PIN attempts on a gallery).
  • To send account emails you'd expect: sign-in codes, receipts, and — only if a studio turns it on — updates about their own events to their own guests.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use guest photos, selfies, or contact details for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers ("sub-processors") to run Frameline. Each only receives the data it needs to do its job:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, file storage, and our database (Workers, R2, D1, Vectorize).
  • Razorpay — payment processing for orders (India).
  • Resend — sending sign-in-code and transactional emails.
  • Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google" to sign in.

We don't share personal data with anyone else, except where required by law.

How long we keep it

  • A studio's account data is kept while the account is active, and for a reasonable period after in case you want to reactivate it.
  • An event's photos and guest data are kept for as long as the studio keeps that event live. When a studio deletes an event (or a photo, or an album), it's held for 30 days so it can be restored by mistake, then permanently removed.
  • A guest's face data (the selfie match data, not the selfie image itself) is deleted with the event it was created for, and a studio can turn face matching off for an event at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent for anything we asked for it. For anything to do with a specific event's guest data, the fastest route is asking that event's studio directly, since they control the gallery; for anything about your own studio account, or if you'd rather come to us, email dev@gratefulworldventures.in and we'll act on it.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences (like a collapsed menu). We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

Frameline is meant for use by event photographers and their adult clients and guests. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children without appropriate consent.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data: dev@gratefulworldventures.in.
